# Figure and schema references from M1 surface.xlsx

This is a review queue created from sheet `M1 surface` in the Kaskan workbook on 2026-10-20. It indexes all 402 rows with a reference where `Kind of data` is not `data`. Rows 470 onward that contain derived interpretation/comparison calculations are retained in `workbook_rows.csv` but are not treated as new figure sources. The earlier figure/schema and source matrix rows resolve to 29 distinct paper candidates in `paper_index.csv`.

`paper_index.csv` gives the exact workbook rows and reference text. Where the paper PDF was already in `papers_to_do/Cortical Image References`, the relative path points there. Additional PDFs were copied from Evo-M1-Trait-Data paper folders or the local EndNote library when available. A blank `pdf_location` means the reference is queued, but no local PDF was found in the inspected holdings. The inventory is a source-review queue; workbook numeric estimates have been removed. Any eventual measurement must come from independent source-PDF work under the Flatmap measurement protocol.

Three case values explicitly printed in the Mayer et al. 2019 Results text were transcribed independently into Evo-M1-Trait-Data; its unlabeled figure-bar estimate was excluded. Its rows 153–160 are figure-derived estimates and appear here for independent flatmap review. The EndNote library PDF for Mayer 2019 was located and copied into this queue; the gap inventory identifies it as record 8372. The [full open article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6703879/) is available for retrieval.
