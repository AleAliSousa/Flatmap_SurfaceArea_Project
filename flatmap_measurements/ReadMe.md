Make a pipeline to measure the surface area of a flatmap. The pipeline will take in a flatmap image and output the calculated surface area in square units.
Optionally, sometimes it will be possible to check the measurement against known values or reference measurements to ensure accuracy. This can be done in a folder called comparison. 
There is one older per source publication, whether or not the meaurement has been published. 
The pipeline will also include a step to visualize the flatmap and the measured surface area, allowing for easy verification of the results in ImageJ or similar software.
