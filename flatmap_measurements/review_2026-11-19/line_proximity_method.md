# Source-line proximity screen

The reproducible script is [`scripts/line_proximity_triage.py`](scripts/line_proximity_triage.py). Its final output is [`line_proximity_all_rois.csv`](line_proximity_all_rois.csv), with the smaller [`line_proximity_flagged.csv`](line_proximity_flagged.csv) for follow-up. The scan ran after the 19 November contour corrections and included every current regional polygon: **1,906 ROIs** in **399 ROI-bearing views**. The project has 461 canonical views across 48 processed publication folders; views without a regional polygon have no edge-distance metric. All 1,906 polygons had a saved source raster. The earlier metrics used for the enlarged-review classifications are retained as [`line_proximity_review_snapshot.csv`](line_proximity_review_snapshot.csv).

For each view, the script uses `source_preview.png`, or `source.tif` when no preview exists. It reduces the longest raster dimension to at most 1,400 pixels. Dark ink means average RGB intensity below 155. Polygon edges are sampled about every two original image pixels. The CSV gives the fraction of sampled points farther than 8 and 15 source pixels from dark ink, median and maximum distance, and the longest contiguous run farther than 8 pixels. The row coordinates identify that run. A monochrome direct polygon is flagged for enlarged review when the run is at least 80 pixels and at least 5% of samples are over 15 pixels from dark ink. The flagged file also includes manually reviewed rows even if they fall below those cutoffs.

This is a **screening signal, not a tracing or anatomical validation**. A printed field border may be light gray, colored, dashed, hidden by labels or tracer, or deliberately interpolated. A nearby dark label can produce a falsely reassuring small distance. Color or tinted source images are identified in the CSV and are not automatically flagged by this threshold. Krubitzer & Kaas (1990) uses polygons clipped by a separate tissue mask; their artificial closure edges can be far from source ink, so those rows are marked `mask_clipped_polygon` and omitted from automatic flags. The `manual_review_status` column records the enlarged checks and corrections. Blank means the ROI was not individually certified.

On the final geometry, 40 rows meet the automatic flag rule; 61 appear in the flagged file after including manually reviewed examples. Every one of those 61 rows has a review disposition: 19 corrected, 31 intentional or false positives, 10 unresolved and retained with provisional or rejected status, and one known off-line FST trace already rejected and excluded from analysis because the source has no defensible closed replacement. The 19 corrections are documented in [`area_changes.csv`](area_changes.csv) and the [visual gallery](index.html). The saved review-snapshot flags and the two classification tables explain the decisions made before the final retracing.
