Full dataset · coverage and contour recheck

16 October 2026 · All 21 papers and 370 numbered figures rechecked against the supplied source pages.

206 additional field/component observations · 269 existing contours refined

79 additions belong to experimental or repeated case views; 127 belong to comparative illustrations. The dataset now contains 1076 contour observations after withdrawing 12 unsupported earlier white-gap candidates. These are not counts of animals or complete cortical areas.

Each paper below provides full source pages with all case overlays, current/source/previous toggles, case labels and editable ImageJ links. Existing per-case tracing.png files continue to match their own ROI sets. Letter-shaped and tracer-gap candidates were corrected or rejected; unresolved field totals remain flagged.

Start with the omissions

  • Beck 1996, figure 2: all four cases together. They were already separate saved views; a single-case tracing image made the other cases look omitted. This pass also adds nine local field outlines and the missing motor-map SR outline.
  • Luethke 1988, figure 3: specimen 85-90. The main reconstruction had been omitted while its inset was retained. Twelve main-map fields and one repeated inset observation are now saved.
  • Krubitzer et al. 1993: bat local maps. Closed SII/PV/VS envelopes and explicitly partial 3b components are recovered without counting repeated panels as new animals.

Complete-field summaries choose one calibrated complete observation per identified animal and field where supported. Partial components, illustrative summaries and unresolved specimen identities remain separate. Unclosed anatomical borders and nonmetric source geometries remain explicit NA; the recheck does not certify every anatomical interpretation.
