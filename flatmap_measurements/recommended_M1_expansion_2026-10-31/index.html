Marmoset: published native-surface areas

Bock Table1 reports motor-complex means of 36 ± 2 mm² left and 37 ± 3 mm² right (n=4). S1 is 28 ± 4 and 30 ± 4 mm², respectively. These are published measurements, not new flatmap tracings. The motor complex includes M1, premotor cortex and frontal eye fields.

Remaining needs and limits

  • Burman: corrected anatomical tracings and uncertainty treatment, plus a corrected figure-scale statement or original metric map. A presumed 1-mm typo correction is not applied.
  • Neafsey: digitize the curved 1-mm² grid and integrate locally; a single global scale would be wrong.
  • Ghosh case9: original figure with a visible 2-mm bar or explicit common-scale evidence.
  • Glasser/Jung: matching native surface meshes and parcel labels, plus somatotopic labels for face-specific measurements.
  • Mouse: the recommended Tennant PDF was not found among these newly added PDFs.
  • Additional fields: Burman’s remaining atlas parcels and Ghosh’s remaining medial/parietal subdivisions are not yet digitized. This batch does not claim exhaustive atlas parcellation. The figure inventory distinguishes these from genuinely unmeasurable boundaries.

Reproducibility and review

Each dataset preserves its PDF, page and crop coordinates, scale endpoints, canonical polygon JSON, source TIFF, editable ImageJ ROI archive, binary masks and source-versus-tracing gallery. Native ImageJ ROI/mask read-back passed on the exported views. Manual anatomical review remains provisional; sampling, projection, tissue distortion and printed-line uncertainty are not captured by the numerical decimal places.

Summary means and sample SD use nonmissing case values within the same paper and region definition. Paired hemispheres are averaged within animal first. Pixel-only values and partial windows are not pooled into physical field means. No totals are formed from overlapping or unmatched components.
