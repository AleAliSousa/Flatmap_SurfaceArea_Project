Brecht alternatives: tracing review

31 October 2026 · Five supplied papers · 19 calibrated target-field tracings, 7 pixel-only response footprints, and 1 repeated-figure check. One additional forehead view (Willoughby D) is explicitly unmeasurable from the print; its published cluster area is preserved.

Use restrictions. Macaque face measurements are 3b face + oral, not Brecht’s putative 3a+3b or external face alone. Human cheek/forehead footprints cannot replace complete S1-face. Calibrated figure areas are 2D estimates; native mesh areas are separate. All anatomical traces remain provisional. No whole-cortex percentages or artificial cortex totals were calculated.

Checks and comparisons

  • Native ImageJ read-back passed for every exported selection: calibration, image pixels, ROI masks and mask stacks. Area equations, missing values, unique keys and file hashes passed. Every processed source/overlay was inspected.
  • Hinds: mean traced V1 2656.0 mm² versus mean of the tabulated native values 2964.0 mm² (-10.4%). The paper reports its rounded mean as 2960 mm². The systematic difference is unresolved; no scale was fitted to the benchmark. Case comparison.
  • Barton S4 right: Figure 2 202.9 versus Figure 3 185.7 mm² (-8.5%). Same tissue; count once. Short printed bars make endpoint placement material; scale-only sensitivity scenarios are saved per calibrated paper.
  • Willoughby: Table 1 forehead cluster areas and derived per-subject sums are kept separate from traced display pixels.
  • Barton supplementary maps and Figure S6 were not supplied. Three of eight hemispheres and the published numerical area check remain unavailable. Non-target auditory fields, digit maps and SII maps are inventoried separately.
