# Rosa_etal_1993

12 numbered figures assessed; 8 views and 107 provisional field/component measurements saved. Counts are observations, including overlapping domains and repeated drawings, not animals.

Six right-hemisphere case maps (figures 5A–10A) and two composite schematics (3C, 11) are measured. Case-map bars are 10 mm, without shrinkage correction; schematics remain pixel-only. Source taxonomy is Cebus apella. Thick tissue/sulcal outlines and black/striped tracer deposits are not substituted for thin areal boundaries. V1 envelopes include their internal sulcal and tracer marks. External POm leaders identify medial compartments in cases 2/3. LIP and MST are combined where subdivisions are not separately traced. Thirty-five case-field boundaries remain needs_anatomical_review with NA area; other absent or non-independent outlines have explicit reasons. These are defensible provisional contours, not a completed anatomical parcellation.

Rebuild from saved geometry with `python scripts/rebuild_all.py --java /path/to/java --ij-jar /path/to/ij.jar`. Python requires numpy, Pillow and pypdfium2; summaries require base R. `python scripts/measure_flatmaps.py --verify` checks saved outputs. Geometry is editable in each `figures/<map_id>/rois.json` or the corresponding native ImageJ ROI ZIP; coordinate changes must be rebuilt before use.
