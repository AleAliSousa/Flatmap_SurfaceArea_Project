Missing-region review — actions completed

28 contours added; one existing contour revised. All 22 listed actions now have recorded outcomes. Open the new before/source/after review and area changes. Original audit observations are retained below for provenance; current images and inventories reflect the corrections.

20 October 2026 · Reusable tracing method · Scope and findings

Inventory: 21 papers, 304 saved views, 1116 ROIs. Visual inspection: 22 selected views across 18 other papers.

The five omission views have been processed, the missing S component inventoried, and candidate borders reassessed. Twenty-seven complete-envelope additions and one approximate V envelope are saved. Illustrative drawings remain excluded from animal means; the approximate V is excluded from complete-field means.

The 1157 saved unmeasured entries include partial fields, contextual labels, unavailable totals and repeated views. They are not 1157 recoverable fields. The fig05 pilot entries have been assessed individually: area 17 was added and unresolved boundaries now have specific source-limit reasons.

This is a targeted visual audit, not a fresh visual certification of all 304 views or all published figures. Canonical ROIs and measured areas were updated for accepted corrections. Scale endpoints and specimen identities were preserved. Kaas (2011) and Zilles (1985) have no saved ROI definitions; their existing exclusion records were read, but their source figures were not re-reviewed here. Felleman (1991) is covered by the preceding correction.
