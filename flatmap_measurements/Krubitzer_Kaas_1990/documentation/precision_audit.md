# Source contour precision audit — 15 October 2026

The previous digitizations were checked against their retained source images. Coarse straight segments and offsets from printed curves were found in a subset of fields. These were fitted with dense local stroke contours, then checked visually. The existing source raster coordinates and scale calibration are preserved; upsampling is not claimed to recover unseen anatomical detail.

Local grayscale ridge tracking was constrained near the old anatomical guide, followed by smoothing of sub-stroke fluctuations, removal of tracking loops and source-image review. Opposite polarity was used for pale drawn borders on dark photographs. Automated tracking did not define the anatomy. Shared borders at affected junctions were explicitly reconciled, and frame-defined partial-view closures were kept at their original coordinates. Obscured/dashed segments remain approximate. Dotted photographic borders and filled schematic summaries retain their documented original conventions; their source limitations prevent a claim of exact continuous-line tracing. Existing dense contours were retained when no source-visible correction was justified.

`precision_audit.csv` records per-region outcomes and limitations. All saved views, including the two 1993 views and one 1990 view without defensible field ROIs, are included in the review page. There are no newly invented fields or absolute calibrations. Partial fields remain partial, missing fields remain missing, and schematic views remain separate from specimen observations.

The original dataset was backed up before edits in `flatmap_measurements/_backups/precision_audit_2026-10-15/`. Previous canonical definitions and measurements are additionally preserved as `rois_before_precision_audit.json` and `measurements_before_precision_audit.csv`. The current `figures/<map_id>/rois.json` remains the authoritative editable geometry. Rebuilding from it regenerates areas and native ImageJ ROI/mask/overlay exports.

The source-toggle review shows previous and current outlines on the same image, with a field selector for enlarging small structures. For the 1990 whole reconstructions, field polygons are clipped to the independent tissue mask; their intentional outside-tissue guide segments are not anatomical contours. Those original tissue masks and their calibration are preserved. Physical area and proportion changes are reported without forcing agreement with published reference ranges. Native and numerical checks establish reproducibility, not anatomical certification.
