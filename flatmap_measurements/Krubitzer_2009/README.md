# Krubitzer_2009

9 numbered figures assessed; 11 views and 109 provisional field/component measurements saved. Counts are observations, including overlapping domains and repeated drawings, not animals.

Figures 4, 5 and 7 yield eleven illustrative views. Figure 4 is unscaled. Figures 5 and 7 use their printed local bars. Motor, parietal and small visual boundaries were inspected at enlargement; CM/MM is retained as a combined field where the printed divider is absent. Named parent domains explicitly include their child fields and are non-additive. Open background regions are NA rather than flood-filled through boundary gaps.

[Source-toggle review](review/precision_review.html) · [All-figure assessment](review/gallery.html) · [Measurements](data/measurements.csv) · [Methods](documentation/methods.md) · [Field inventory](documentation/field_inventory.csv)

Rebuild from saved geometry with `python scripts/rebuild_all.py --java /path/to/java --ij-jar /path/to/ij.jar`. Python requires numpy, Pillow and pypdfium2; summaries require base R. `python scripts/measure_flatmaps.py --verify` checks saved outputs. Geometry is editable in each `figures/<map_id>/rois.json` or the corresponding native ImageJ ROI ZIP; coordinate changes must be rebuilt before use.
