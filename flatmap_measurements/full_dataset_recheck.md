# Full dataset coverage and contour recheck · 16 October 2026

All 21 publications and 370 numbered figures were rechecked from the supplied source figures and captions. This is a renewed source review, not a claim that every possible region in every reduced comparative drawing has a reliable complete area. Source-limited or unresolved totals remain explicit NA.

Start with [the interactive review](review_full_dataset_audit.html). It displays every traced specimen together on its original source page, with current/source/previous toggles and editable ImageJ links. The [paper ledger](full_dataset_recheck_papers.csv) separates case-view additions from comparative illustrations; the [contour ledger](full_dataset_recheck_contours.csv) identifies every added/refined field. No observation count is an animal count.

The omitted main reconstruction for squirrel 85-90 (Luethke 1988 figure 3) is now measured. Local maps previously rejected wholesale in Beck 1996, Huffman 1999, Krubitzer et al. 1993 and 1995 were reconsidered field by field. Unknown owl identities in Krubitzer–Kaas 1993 figure 2 remain unresolved. The Beck figure 2 per-case files already covered all four cases; their new combined source view makes that coverage visible without changing the meaning of each native per-case ROI set.

Existing staircase contours were resampled and regularized, then constrained to their nearby printed anatomical stroke. Source-following candidates were inspected at enlarged scale. Fits that followed lettering, stipple, recording symbols or an interior patch were rejected or manually retraced. Tight-notch self-crossings were repaired conservatively and shared boundaries reconciled. Reduced illustrations receive selected supported outlines; dense/merged or topographically ambiguous envelopes remain unquantified. This limitation is explicit rather than inventing a complete border.

A late enlarged-source check withdrew twelve older bat-paper candidates that followed white gaps between tracer marks. Clear SII envelopes and interrupted source curves were retraced; unresolved perimeters remain NA. Withdrawn labels are listed in the contour ledger and the bat-paper withdrawal record.

All modified native selections and masks are rebuilt in ImageJ and checked by readback, including TIFF calibration and area agreement. Polygon bounds, labels, self-crossings and interior overlaps are audited. These checks establish reproducibility and geometric consistency; they do not certify anatomical field assignment. Current JSON is canonical, and earlier geometry/native outputs are archived under `_backups/full_dataset_review_2026-10-16/`.

Absolute complete-field summaries select one supported complete calibrated observation per identified specimen and literal field, preferring main maps and using a repeated local map only as fallback. Partial components, comparative drawings and unresolved identities are excluded. No cross-publication pooling or additional whole-neocortex denominator is introduced. Unscaled valid outlines retain pixel geometry. Projected brain drawings, sections and flatmaps with uncorrected nonuniform distortion are not promoted to true cortical surfaces.

Paper-specific findings and remaining limits are linked from every source review. Earlier batch reviews and their counts are historical snapshots.

## Annotation correction

Interior letters, numbers and recording/stimulation symbols have no effect on area. They are not exclusions or anatomical features. Figure 12A in Beck et al. (1996) received a further correction after user review: the SR edge now continues smoothly across the `78 / El,S` and `38 / T` overprints. See [the correction record](Beck_etal_1996/documentation/fig12A_label_correction.md).
