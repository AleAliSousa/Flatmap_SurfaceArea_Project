Brecht alternatives: human and macaque

Literature screening, 30 October 2026. These are candidate sources, not newly traced measurements. The strongest complete map-plus-area matches are V1 and auditory fields. No complete human S1-face area plus calibrated flatmap was verified.

Definition constraint: Brecht Fig. 3 uses putative 3a + 3b in human and macaque. Record 3b alone, 3a+3b, and 3a+3b+1+2 separately. Record external face versus face plus oral structures separately. A scale bar cannot correct local flattening distortion. Do not borrow Brecht's scale.
