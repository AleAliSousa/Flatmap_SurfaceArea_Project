M1 literature screening for the species cohorts

31 October 2026 · Recommendations only; no measurements added or workbook values changed.

Cohort source: /Users/crossmodal/Library/CloudStorage/OneDrive-AllenInstitute/Species/Evo-M1-Trait-Data/_keys/species_cohorts.csv. EvoM1 has 25 species including human. The file names its four-species human/rhesus/marmoset/mouse set HMBA; no separate BICAN cohort is defined here.

First acquisition priorities: Burman 2008, Remple 2006, Tennant 2011 and Neafsey 1986. For recent or explicitly quantified partial maps, add Halley 2025, Dea 2016 and Bergeron 2026. For human/rhesus whole-M1 area, prefer measuring labelled native surfaces over tracing a printed flat display.

“High priority” is a literature-screening judgment, not certification that every figure has a usable scale. New candidates were checked against local filenames and the existing merged M1 surface-area sources; that is not an exhaustive full-text duplicate audit. Sources marked already held should not be counted as new literature. A gap means no suitable source verified in this search, not proof none exists.

Measurement rule: retain anatomical M1, stimulation-defined movement territory, and body-part subregions as separate variables. Use same-panel physical calibration for planar maps; for digital flatmaps use native surface areas or documented local distortion correction. Keep hemisphere, tissue shrinkage, specimen identity and species provenance attached to each estimate.
